# Product positioning

SleepAgent turns longitudinal sleep observations into understandable evidence and bounded care coordination. Its users are an older adult, authorized family members, and clinicians. The product emphasizes explanation, uncertainty, consent, and follow-up rather than diagnosis or autonomous treatment.

The radar-first experience is a product input, not an agent identity. Vendor integrations, device quality checks, retrieval, reports, and persistence remain services or tools around the closed four-agent runtime.
